1. Roster / Art brief 原文

身份写在关卡名与名单第一句里——Critic 只是把缺席说穿。

roster LEVELS_ROSTER.md · ruins — Bayon Ruins

An overgrown stone temple complex at dawn in ground mist. Carved faces, collapsed galleries, roots prising apart masonry, moss and lichen, standing water in courtyards. Soft low-angle light through mist with long god rays between towers. The quietest level in the roster — its power is atmosphere and scale, not intensity. Stone needs real weathering: water staining, biological growth, chipped edges, differential erosion. — LEVELS_ROSTER.md · ruins 条目（名单表也写 grey-gold / moss · dawn · ground mist）

全局 Instant-fail（贴图 / 几何）

  • Any surface that is flat, untextured, or single-colour
  • Geometry with no silhouette detail — boxes standing in for objects
  • Perfectly clean, straight, or uniform anything
  • A level that photographs well in one pose and is empty everywhere else
  • Reusing another level's palette or lighting recipe

另见 ARCHITECTURE.md §9 Quality bar

Critic 的杀伤力来自 brief 与画面的可核对缺口： 名单以 carved faces 起头，关卡以 Bayon 命名，五个 framing 里却一张脸都没有。 这不是品味分歧，是合同违约。

Your critic's verdict names something different from every other level in the build: "The Bayon face. Everything else on this list is a defect; this one is the level's identity failing." 1. THERE ARE NO CARVED FACES IN ANY OF THE FIVE PUBLISHED FRAMINGS. The roster brief LEADS with "carved faces". MASONRY … still pristine 90-degree cuboids … apply reliefBox more widely. Moss, lichen and vertical water staining DO work; keep them. 4. hero1's grade_split … You have 95 draws and 2.83M triangles spare … A carved face can be expensive.

塔身 / 砌石轮廓
浮雕高度场网格
缺失的脸（R3）
凹处（眼窝/唇缝）

示意：连续高度场 + 轮廓出石，而不是“贴一张脸贴图”。 真实实现是 3D 浮雕三角网，见 faceHeight。

关键注册错误（曾把脸“埋进石头”）

错误：对 mid 半宽注册

塔身向上收分。对脸中部半宽取 inset 时， 下巴/嘴/鼻/眼都落在石头内部，只有冠部露出 → 远看像顶部小饰。

正确：对 foot 半宽注册

用脸底部（最宽）半宽；整头站出塔面，越往上越相对突出——与真 Bayon 一致。

// level_ruins.js — face placement (concept)
wantFace → fy, fh, hwBot / hwTop
fw = min(hwMid * 1.86, fh * 1.10)
oR = hwBot - inset          // register at FOOT
fdp = fw * 0.160            // relief depth
// then sample faceHeight(u,v) → displace along face normal

6. 贴图 / Wear 如何咬合浮雕

只有 mesh 凸起不够：切口与脸颊同深时，深度 AO 看不见唇缝——要贴图合同里的 concavity。

深度能看见的

  • 眼窝相对脸前平面后退
  • 鼻下 / 下颌 moat
  • 头外缘贴塔的角遮挡

深度看不见、靠 concavity 的

  • 两唇之间的切口
  • 眼睑缝
  • 同深度上的雕刻线
Fix 把局部凹度（高度场模糊 − 高度场）写入顶点色： 凹 → 拉高 grime / wet（变暗）；凸棱 → 偏淡 spall—— proud pale forms against dark cuts，仅靠天光也能读脸。 这是 mesh + wear 贴图合同的联合，不是换一张 albedo 脸贴图。 
// _paint on carved face (concept from level_ruins.js)
deep = saturate((F - oz) / dp)           // behind front plane
concavity = blur(height) - height        // local cuts
→ darken R (grime) and G (wet) in sockets / incisions
→ pale B-ish substrate on brow arris / lid rim / lip

与“只调 UV / 噪声”的区别

做法结果
只在塔上铺更强石材噪声 仍是光秃塔；20 m 外无 Bayon 剪影
平面四边形 + 脸部 albedo 贴图 侧光无浮雕；违背“雕刻”brief；易 tiling / 卡片感
高度场浮雕网格 + wear 凹处 + 石材 PBR 轮廓与光影同时工作；贴图负责风化，几何负责身份
